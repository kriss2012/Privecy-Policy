1. Introduction

Kiri Store — Official Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 9, 2026 | Document ID: KS-PP-2026-V1 | Contact: 202krishnapatil@gmail.com

Compliance & Legal Transparency Notice

This Privacy Policy details the data management architecture of Kiri Store. Regulatory references (GDPR, CCPA, COPPA, and Google Play Store policies) articulate our intended operational alignment with international privacy benchmarks. Where third-party service integrations or regional transfers occur, organizational reviews and data processing agreements govern those workflows.

Section 01

1. Introduction

Welcome to Kiri Store ("we," "us," "our," or "Company"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process your information in connection with our mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing and using our Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the provisions of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our Services.

Section 02

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Registration: When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and password.
  • Profile Information: Content you provide in your profile, including profile picture, bio, and preferences.
  • Communications: Any messages, inquiries, feedback, reviews, or support requests you submit to us.
  • Payment Information: If you make purchases, we collect billing address, payment method, and transaction history. Payment processing is handled by third-party payment processors who maintain PCI DSS compliance.
  • Content: Any photos, videos, documents, or other content you upload or create through our Services.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, device settings, and device performance data.
  • Usage Data: Pages or features you access, time spent in the application, searches, taps, clicks, scrolling, content interactions, and crash reports.
  • Location Information: Precise and approximate location data collected via GPS, Bluetooth, and Wi-Fi signals (only with your explicit permission).
  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, referring/exit pages, access times, and error information.
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to recognize you and maintain your preferences.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from external third-party sources, including:

  • Social media platforms (if you connect your social account)
  • Analytics providers and ad networks
  • Data brokers and public databases
  • Business partners and service providers
  • Other users who provide information about you

2.4 Sensitive Personal Information

Our Services may collect the following sensitive categories of data, subject to strict purpose limitation and explicit user consent:

Biometric data (fingerprint, face recognition — only with explicit consent)
Health and fitness data (if applicable)
Financial account information
Authentication credentials
Precise location data
Legal & Architecture Review Note: Biometric authentication (e.g. Android fingerprint/Face Unlock) is handled on-device via native OS APIs and biometric hashes are not transmitted or stored on Kiri Store remote servers. Maintainers should confirm if any fitness or financial data features are currently enabled.

3.1 Service Delivery & Improvement

3.3 Marketing & Advertising

  • Conducting surveys and market research

  • Measuring advertising effectiveness

3.4 Safety & Legal Compliance

  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud and security incidents
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service and other agreements
  • Protecting the rights, property, and safety of our Company, users, and the public
  • Complying with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes
  • Investigating and resolving disputes

3.5 Analytics & Aggregation

  • Analyzing usage patterns and trends
  • Creating aggregated data for statistical purposes
  • Improving decision-making and business operations
Section 04

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

4.1 Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information with service providers who perform services on our behalf, including:

  • Cloud hosting and storage providers
  • Payment processors and financial institutions
  • Analytics and data analysis providers
  • Email and communication service providers
  • Customer support platforms
  • Marketing and advertising partners
Contractual Safeguards: All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information, maintain confidentiality, and use it only for the verified business purposes we specify in written agreements.

4.2 Business Transfers

If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, dissolution, reorganization, or similar transaction or proceeding, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change and any choices you may have regarding your information.

4.3 Legal Requirements & Safety

We may disclose your information when required by law or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes
  • Respond to lawful government requests
  • Protect against fraud, security, or technical issues
  • Protect the rights, property, and safety of our Company, users, and the public

4.4 With Your Consent

We may share your information with other third parties when you provide explicit consent to do so.

4.5 Public Content

Any information you voluntarily post publicly within our Services (comments, reviews, profile information) may be viewed by other users and may be indexed by search engines.

Important Transparency Commitment: We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. However, we do share information with analytics and advertising partners for the legitimate operational purposes described in this policy.
Section 05

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, organizational, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption: Strong cryptographic protocols for all data in transit.
  • At-Rest Encryption: Encryption protocols safeguarding sensitive data stored within our infrastructure.
  • Secure Authentication: Industry-standard authentication mechanisms and credential hashing.
  • Regular Assessments: Continuous security evaluations and scheduled penetration testing.
  • Access Governance: Strict role-based permissions and least-privilege administrative controls.
  • Workforce Oversight: Employee training on data privacy protocols and cybersecurity best practices.
  • Incident Response: Documented rapid response procedures for investigating potential data security anomalies.
Security Reality & User Responsibility: While we strive to protect your information using reasonable security measures, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.
Section 06

6. Your Rights & Choices

6.1 Access & Portability

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and to receive a copy in a portable format.

6.2 Correction & Update

You can update, correct, or modify your account information at any time through your account settings or by contacting us.

6.3 Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal and contractual obligations. Once deleted, we may retain aggregated or anonymized data.

6.4 Opt-Out & Unsubscribe

  • Marketing Communications: You can opt out of marketing emails, push notifications, and promotional messages by clicking "unsubscribe" in emails or adjusting notification settings in the app.
  • Cookies: You can manage cookies through your browser settings.
  • Location Tracking: You can disable location permissions in your device settings.

6.5 Advertising Preferences

You can adjust personalized advertising settings in your device's advertising preferences or opt out of interest-based advertising through industry opt-out tools.

6.6 Do Not Track

Some browsers include a Do Not Track feature. Our Services currently do not respond to DNT signals, but you can use other tools to limit tracking as described above.

6.7 Regulatory Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional statutory rights, including:

  • The right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting past lawful processing
  • The right to restrict processing of personal information
  • The right to object to processing on legitimate interest grounds
  • The right to lodge a complaint with an applicable supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Privacy Team at 202krishnapatil@gmail.com.

Section 07

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods vary depending on the type of data and the purposes for which we process it:

Account Information Duration + 90 Days Maintained while active; erased within 90 days after account deletion.
Transaction Records Minimum 7 Years Retained to fulfill statutory corporate, tax, and accounting mandates.
Marketing Preferences Until User Opt-Out Retained until consent is revoked or communication opt-out is received.
Usage & Analytics Data Typically 2 Years Aggregated or purged after 24 months of telemetry analysis.
Support Communications 3 Years Maintained for service quality, resolution tracking, and dispute verification.

After the retention period expires, we securely delete or anonymize your information. However, we may retain some data in backup copies for a limited period, though this data will not be actively used.

Section 08

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

8.1 For Teens (Ages 13–18)

If you are between 13 and 18, you have additional rights regarding your personal information. You can request deletion of your content, and we provide enhanced privacy settings for your age group. Parents or guardians may contact us to review, update, or delete their child's information.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at 202krishnapatil@gmail.com.

Section 09

9. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

9.1 Cookies

We use cookies (small text files stored on your device) to enhance your experience by:

  • Maintaining your login session
  • Remembering your preferences
  • Analyzing usage patterns
  • Delivering personalized content and advertisements

9.2 Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic platform functionality, authentication, and security.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our Services through aggregated telemetry.
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your interface preferences, language choices, and customizations.
  • Targeting/Advertising Cookies: Deliver personalized advertisements and evaluate campaign efficacy.

9.3 Third-Party Tracking

Third-party service providers (such as Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, and advertising networks) may place cookies and tracking pixels on our Services to collect information about your behavior. These third parties have their own privacy policies.

Vendor Audit Note: Maintainers should review whether Facebook Pixel or third-party ad network tags are actively loaded in the production build or whether privacy-preserving cookieless telemetry is preferred for GitHub store distribution.

9.4 Cookie Management

You can control cookies through:

  • Browser settings (most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies)
  • Cookie preference centers on our Services
  • Industry opt-out tools (www.aboutads.info, www.iab.com)
Impact of Disabling Cookies: Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact the operational functionality and authentication flows of our Services.
Section 10

10. International Data Transfer

Our Services may be provided from, and your information may be transferred to, countries other than where you reside. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from your country of origin.

By using our Services, you consent to the transfer of your information to countries outside your country of residence, which may not have the same data protection laws. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards such as:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): European Commission-approved model contract terms for international transfers.
  • Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs): Internal codes of conduct for enterprise cross-border transfers.
  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to jurisdictions formally recognized as providing equivalent legal protection.
  • Explicit Consent: Explicit user authorization where applicable under statutory exceptions.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or other operational factors. We will notify you of material changes by:

  • Requesting your explicit consent if required by applicable data protection laws

Your continued use of our Services after changes become effective constitutes your acknowledgement and acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Direct Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based exclusively on the verified terms set forth in this Privacy Policy.

Does Kiri Store sell my personal data?
No. As affirmed in Section 4.5 of our policy, we do not sell your personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. Data sharing is limited to essential service providers (such as cloud hosting, analytics, and payment processors) under binding confidentiality agreements.
How can I request deletion of my data?
Pursuant to Section 6.3, you can request permanent deletion of your personal records at any time. Simply send an email with your account details to our Privacy Team at 202krishnapatil@gmail.com. Upon verification, your account data will be purged within 90 days, subject only to required statutory tax or accounting retentions.
How long do you retain my information?
Under Section 7, retention periods correspond strictly to operational necessity: active account data is retained for account duration plus up to 90 days post-deletion; transaction and financial records for a minimum of 7 years for tax compliance; analytics telemetry for typically 2 years; and customer support communications for 3 years.
How are financial and payment details handled?
As outlined in Section 2.1, all billing and payment method information is processed directly by certified third-party payment processors who maintain full PCI DSS compliance. Kiri Store does not store raw credit card numbers on its servers.
Are children allowed to use the Services?
Under Section 8, our Services are not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If parental notice is received, such records are deleted promptly. Users aged 13 to 18 are provided enhanced privacy settings and parental deletion channels.
How do I opt out of marketing communications?
Under Section 6.4, you may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link included at the bottom of every marketing email, or by adjusting notification preferences in your application settings.
